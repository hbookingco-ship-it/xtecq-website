Our Process

3-Tier Quality Assurance

Every dataset we deliver passes through a rigorous, multi-layer QA pipeline engineered to maintain 99%+ accuracy at scale.

1

Tier 1: Primary Annotation

Vetted native-speaker annotators complete initial labeling tasks using standardized guidelines, custom tooling, and client-defined taxonomies.

2

Tier 2: Expert QA Review

Senior reviewers with domain expertise cross-validate annotations, flagging discrepancies and ensuring guideline adherence with automated inter-annotator agreement scoring.

3

Tier 3: Final Accuracy Audit

A dedicated QA Lead conducts a final blind audit on randomized samples, verifying accuracy benchmarks before secure dataset delivery to the client.

Our Quality Guarantee

If any delivered batch falls below the agreed accuracy threshold, we re-annotate at no additional cost. No questions asked.
